PTB-XL contains about 21,799 clinical 12-lead ECGs from 18,869 patients, with 10-second recordings.

MIMIC-IV-ECG contains about 800,000 diagnostic ECGs across nearly 160,000 patients, also with 12 leads and 10-second length at 500 Hz.
